How to use

  • Type to carry on, as in any chat.
  • Click a sentence, or highlight any words, to ask about just that part. The thread opens beside it.
  • Continue from here under an earlier answer branches off there and folds what came after.
  • Dotted words show a one-line definition on hover.
  • In a diagram, click a box or a link to ask about it.
  • Click a formula to ask about it, or a symbol inside it to ask about just that symbol.
  • Explain it back, under the message box, turns the box into one for your own explanation. Claude's check of it joins the thread.
  • To study an answer you got in another Claude chat, ask Claude there to send it to this page. It opens here as a new conversation.
